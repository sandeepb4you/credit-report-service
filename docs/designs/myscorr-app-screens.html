Build notes for the team
① The four journeys share one design system: teal = action, amber = payment/premium moments, green = healthy, red = risk. Keep this colour grammar consistent — users learn it fast.  ② Instrument these funnel events from day one: otp_verified → payment_success → pan_submitted → score_revealed → loan_offer_viewed → application_submitted → disbursal.  ③ Screens S5 (paywall), S7 (PAN consent), S13 (loan offer) and S15/S16 (referral disclosures) carry regulatory copy — treat that text as locked strings requiring compliance sign-off before release.  ④ Android & iOS share identical flows; platform differences are limited to OTP autofill mechanics, payment intent handling, and native share sheets on S11.
